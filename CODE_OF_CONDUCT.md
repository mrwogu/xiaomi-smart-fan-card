# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Xiaomi Fan Card welcoming and
harassment-free for everyone, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender
identity, experience, nationality, race, religion, or sexual orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respect different viewpoints and experiences.
- Accept constructive criticism professionally.
- Focus on what benefits the project and its users.
- Protect private information.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory or sexualized language.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Deliberate disruption of project discussions or automation.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer profile
[@mrwogu](https://github.com/mrwogu). Do not include private Home Assistant
data or device credentials.

This code of conduct is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
